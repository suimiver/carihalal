Hanya yang disahkan JAKIM

Certified by JAKIM only — nothing else.

Setiap restoran dalam CariHalal telah disemak satu persatu dengan MYeHALAL, direktori halal rasmi JAKIM (Jabatan Kemajuan Islam Malaysia). Jika kedai tiada dalam direktori rasmi, ia tiada dalam aplikasi ini — tiada tekaan, tiada penyenaraian pengisytiharan sendiri. Pensijilan ditunjukkan mengikut premis: badan pensijilan, tarikh luput, dan tarikh pengesahan.

Every restaurant in CariHalal was checked one by one against MYeHALAL, the official halal directory of JAKIM. If a shop is not in the official directory, it is not in this app — no guesses, no self-declared listings. Certification is shown per premises: certificate body, expiry date, and the date the listing was verified.
